AxetosOS.Products.NES v2.22.0 - exact MMC1 CHR-read provenance

Diagnostic-only release.

The v2.21 trace proves sprite zero itself is stable and that missed hits occur only
when the background under the same opaque sprite pixels is transparent. Because
PPUCTRL=$90 routes background patterns through $1000 and MMC1 control=$1E selects
4 KiB CHR mode, that background comes through the actively switched CHR1 bank.

This patch records MMC1's exact compiled PPU read-complete mapping/data and
correlates it with RP2C02 background pattern fetches on sprite-zero scanlines
17-24. No CPU/PPU/MMC1/compiler execution behavior changes.

Expected test total remains 266.
