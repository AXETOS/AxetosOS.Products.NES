AxetosOS.Products.NES v2.23.0 - physical cartridge PPU-bus topology correction

Behavior/topology release.

The v2.22 Alien Syndrome trace showed 544/544 exact MMC1 CHR-read matches in
each sprite-zero background window, so the simple 4 KiB CHR bank decoder was
not randomly returning the wrong byte. Source inspection then exposed the real
physical-boundary error: replaceable cartridges were connected directly to the
RP2C0x multiplexed AD0-AD7 + ALE bus and implemented a duplicate private low
address latch, even though the console motherboard already owns the SN74LS373.

v2.23.0 moves the cartridge connector to separate PPU A0-A13 and PPU D0-D7
buses. A0-A7 come from motherboard SN74LS373 Q outputs, A8-A13 remain direct
PPU high-address traces, D0-D7 remain on the RP2C0x/CIRAM data traces, and ALE
stays motherboard-local. NROM/MMC1 physical and compiled targets now consume
that same corrected connector topology.

No claim is made that Alien Syndrome is visually fixed until tested locally.
Expected test total remains 266.
