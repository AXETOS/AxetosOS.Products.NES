AxetosOS Products / NES v1.4.4

Profiler/benchmark-guided PPU chip-internal performance sweep.

- Rolls back the measured-slower v1.4.3 HM6116, SN74LS373 and PPU output-stage branch experiments.
- Caches PPUCTRL/PPUMASK decoded internal control lines at register-write time.
- Skips rendering-stage work during post-render/vblank.
- Avoids visible pixel-mux/DAC work on non-visible fetch dots.
- Merges sprite pixel priority selection and sprite output-unit advancement into one pass.
- Preserves all physical motherboard delivery, package pins, VRAM bus transactions, clocking and chip-owned semantics.

Expected tests: 215.
Benchmark against v1.4.2: Mario 22.55 FPS, Donkey Kong 21.04 FPS.
