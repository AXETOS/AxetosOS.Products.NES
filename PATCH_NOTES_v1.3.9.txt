AxetosOS Products / NES v1.3.9

Profiler-driven electrical transport hot-path sweep.

The v1.3.8 profile measured hundreds of millions of net resolutions and billions of physical pin deliveries in short runs. v1.3.9 therefore optimizes transportation itself without teaching the motherboard anything about CPU/PPU/RAM semantics.

Key changes:
- AnyChange inputs: changed electrical level -> chip wake directly; no unused edge counter/divider work.
- Edge/divider counters remain on RisingEdge/FallingEdge pins.
- Dedicated two-driver electrical resolver preserves drive strength, floating, Unknown and Contention behavior.
- Dedicated fast normal ResolveAndPresent/receiver path contains no profiler timing branches.
- HM6116 retains output drive/release state and checks power only when a power pin changed.
- SN74LS373 checks power only when a power pin changed.
- No signal queue, scheduler, settle loop, harmonic clock skipping or chip-semantic motherboard gating.
